and positions; this fixes a cached-versus-fresh pan pixel mismatch at this laptop's
actual 160% scale.

## Changes and decisions

- `viewer/canvas.ts`: prepare replacement tiles within the existing time budget,
  retaining the stretched completed layer until all visible replacement tiles are
  ready. Pans on an already presented scale still reuse visible tiles normally.
- Retain up to two recent scales and at most 16 million additional tile pixels
  (64 MB), beyond the active front/back layers. Scene changes clear those pixels;
  old image/layer/selection/theme/font/drawing versions cannot become cache hits.
  Interrupted replacements preserve the last completed layer.
- Keep the existing high-DPI worker eligibility, four-worker bound, batching,
  source geometry, image transforms and 120 ms quiet interval. No payload/schema
  or converter changes.
- Quantize tile steps and cached tile placement in device pixels. At DPR 1.6,
  the old 256-CSS-pixel step was 409.6 device pixels. Both the old and new
  implementations initially failed the unchanged cached/fresh diagonal-pan
  assertion at that scale; the device-pixel grid passes it.
- `scripts/repaint-check.js` watches the canvases actually composited on each
  draw. It checks no partial replacement presentation, unchanged 500 ms fresh
  deadline, 250 ms cached return including easing, zero cached-return builds,
  cache pixel/count limits, invalidation/release, pan and interrupted zoom.
- Located the last explicit pre-Windows-report acceptance at `672ffb3` in session
  `01a17917-d874-7b72-8d7d-1c0537821400`. Built that full revision in the isolated
  `/tmp/cadkit-repaint-baseline-672ffb3` worktree and used a separate browser
  profile/server on 4298. It emits the same D4 counts: 152,702 primitives and
  1,507,669 hatch segments. Its DPR 1.6 trace still had four missing tiles after
  1,872.7 ms. An additional renderer-only comparison on today's payload at DPR 1
  completed at 586.9 ms. This baseline also presents tiles progressively; it does
  not establish the exact previously fast camera/drawing interaction and does
  not justify a rollback.
- Current s11 reproduction: DPR 1 presented eight incomplete sharp frames before
  completion at 341 ms; DPR 2 presented a 13-of-16-tile frame and completed at
  459.1 ms. The retained host-path DPR 1 probe completed at 288.9 ms with no
  partial replacement frames. These are individual software-browser samples.
- Discarded the normal-scale worker experiment: it improved one normal-scale
  redraw to 213.1 ms, but the existing synthetic image/stroke comparison found
  interior channel delta 3 above its limit of 2, even with host image sampling.
  Normal-scale drawing retains the original host path and its first-time redraw
  cost. The earlier performance gain is not part of the retained change.
- Discarded six/eight-worker and OffscreenCanvas composition experiments. They
  did not reliably improve latency: observed 539.4 ms repeat and 546.6 ms fresh
  redraw failures, and a four-worker Offscreen composition run missed the
  original short-wheel deadline. The retained worker module/pool and bitmap
  composition are the s10 implementation. The original timeouts and fidelity limits were not changed.
