# 2026-10-25 / s09 — Wheel zoom stretches cached tiles

Outcome: O6 · State: active
Source: this change · Environment: Linux x64, headless Chromium with `--disable-gpu`, Node 25.8

## Result

Wheel zoom on the 43.7k-entity Chunar drawing no longer re-renders linework on each frame. Cached
tiles are stretched while the wheel moves, then redrawn sharp at the final scale. Selection is still in the
tile cache key, so a click still repaints every tile. The ~152k-primitive target drawing and a
real GPU browser are unmeasured.

## Changes and decisions

- [`viewer/canvas.ts`](../viewer/canvas.ts): raster tiles live in a `RasterLayer` with its own
  scale. During wheel input and for 120 ms after it (`ZOOM_SETTLE_MS`), nothing is rendered: the
  front layer is drawn scaled, however far the gesture goes. When input settles the front layer
  is rebuilt at the final scale. The previous layer stays
  underneath, stretched, while the new one fills in at up to 8 ms per frame (`RENDER_BUDGET_MS`,
  at least one tile per frame), so the redraw cost is spread across frames and no blank areas
  appear.
- Rejected: rebuilding mid-gesture once the stretch passed 0.5× or 2×. On long scrolls it rendered
  tiles at scales that were stale a few frames later and showed blank edges when zooming out; at
  DPR 2, 117 of 342 frames went over 20 ms and the user saw the lag.
- An overview (the whole drawing at about 2048 device px, no selection) is rendered with
  `requestIdleCallback` after the view settles. It is drawn under the stretched layers wherever they
  have no tiles, so zooming out mid-gesture shows the drawing at the edges instead of blank. When the
  front layer is rebuilt before it has filled in, the complete layer is kept as the stand-in.
- Labels fade instead of vanishing: text used to be skipped below 3 px on screen, so one wheel notch
  on the Chunar drawing (0.41 → 0.50 units/px, 1.27-unit labels at 3.1 → 2.5 px) took 1,106 labels
  down to 4. Text now fades from full opacity at 3 px (`TEXT_FULL_PX`) to nothing at 1.5 px
  (`TEXT_MIN_PX`). Full-view render time at 0.41–1.5 units/px stayed at 20–28 ms; at fit it went
  from 86 ms to 74 ms (noise; only 54 labels there).
- Progressive fill happens only in frames scheduled by `request()`. A direct `draw()` call still
  renders the whole viewport at once, so screenshots and checks stay deterministic.
- Wheel easing time constant 40 → 25 ms: frames are cheap now, so the lag was the easing.
- Zoom buttons and fit rebuild at once, but the old layer still covers gaps while the new
  one fills.
- [`scripts/viewer-check.js`](../scripts/viewer-check.js) follows the new fields. It now asserts
  that the wheel renders no tiles mid-gesture, including on a 12-notch scroll past 4×, and that the
  view re-renders at the final scale once input settles.
