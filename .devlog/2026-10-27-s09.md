## Changes and decisions

- `sidecar/Patterns.cs`: retain the s06 clipping calculation, reuse sorted parameter
  buffers instead of allocating iterator chains/lists per midpoint, and prepare a
  small look-ahead of independent continuous hatches on at most four workers.
- `sidecar/Program.cs`: collect reachable patterned hatches, then apply the existing
  density/segment budgets serially in original emission order. Dashed hatches keep
  their existing path. Shared block definitions reuse their source clipping result.
  Prefetch also checks density before clipping, and releases skipped/consumed results.
- Include the s06 helper, attribution, continuous-hatch fixture and tests as required
  dependencies. No updater changes belong to this performance checkpoint.
- `viewer/canvas.ts`, `viewer/main.ts`: explicitly reuse compiled geometry and hit
  indexes for revalidated payload objects, retaining at most two prepared drawings.
  Theme changes invalidate the cache. Default `setDrawing` still rebuilds for callers
  editing objects in place; new/replacement payload objects compile normally.
- New `pattern_pipeline.dxf` generation/test covers a shared block, nested islands,
  a future pattern too dense to clip, and a dashed family. The fixture is generated,
  not committed. `scripts/prepared-drawing-check.js` checks repeat reuse, theme
  colours, in-place edits, replacement payloads and bounded retention.
