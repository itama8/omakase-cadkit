## Changes and decisions

- `src/file-picker.ts`: show and activate a transparent, borderless topmost owner
  before either Windows dialog. Previously the owner was never shown. No taskbar
  button is added; the owner is disposed on selection/cancel. Native proof pending.
- `viewer/canvas.ts`: prepare paths, primitive bounds and hit indexes in small
  tasks on a separate builder; install completed data atomically. Cancellation
  preserves the live drawing. Keep the synchronous API for existing callers and
  worker compilation. The viewer opts into a bounded initial render and waits for
  the completed frame, including when animation frames are suspended.
- `viewer/main.ts`: yield after payload parsing, await preparation, and retain
  opening feedback through checks and images. Apply the sheet/hash camera before
  reporting completion. Readiness now includes the displayed frame. Add geometry
  readiness to timing details. Parsing itself can still block briefly.
- Replace the idle full-drawing zoom preview render with a bounded snapshot of
  completed tiles. Profiling found a roughly 0.9 s delayed browser stall from the
  old preview even after opening finished. The snapshot covers the completed
  viewport; newly exposed regions still use the normal complete tile rendering.
  Preview generation skips selections so highlights cannot be retained as ink.
- `sidecar/Patterns.cs` and `Program.cs`: dashed hatches join the existing bounded
  preparation queue, retaining the exact ACadSharp clipping, dash
  phases, serial output order and drawing-wide budget. Queue up to sixteen hatches
  with at most four active workers: cheap intervening hatches previously left
  workers idle. This can retain up to sixteen bounded hatch results. Payload/schema
  unchanged.
- Reject the experimental use of the continuous clipper for dashed patterns:
  it changed 42 D5 primitives. Retained preparation instead produces exactly equal
  full D4 and D5 payloads, including entity bounds, primitives and warnings.
- Extend `loading-recovery-check.js` for delayed checks, final sheet readiness,
  preparation cancellation/bounds and preview generation without geometry replay.
