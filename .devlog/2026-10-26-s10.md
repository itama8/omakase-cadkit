## Result

Wrapped title blocks, embedded table presentations and logos, leader arrowheads,
wide polylines, opaque solids, simple dash patterns and bounded hatch textures now
reach both the canvas viewer and SVG export. Missing/unreadable images and reported
unsupported or approximated content appear in a status-bar popover. Reader emission
errors are included rather than silently losing an entity.

Seven selected trial DWGs were freshly read: no emission errors, unresolved raster
references or entries in `unrendered`. This is extraction evidence, not a claim that
all geometry, fonts or plot appearance match. Representative title-block, index-table
and key-plan crops were compared with issued PDFs and inspected in the actual viewer.
The source DWG embeds a different logo revision from its PDF; the viewer preserves
that source picture. Trial drawings, exports and comparisons remain local and are not
repository fixtures.

## Changes and decisions

- Schema 8 carries measured text widths and styled runs, fill modes, disjoint hatch
  segments, dash patterns and embedded image data. Width fitting prevents the chosen
  screen font from expanding a title-block line past its laid-out width.
- Split the sidecar into `Program.cs`, `Text.cs` and `Ole.cs`. The OLE reader handles
  compound streams, cached EMF and EMF carried in WMF chunks, vector table records,
  and DIB bitmaps. Two-pass SRCPAINT/SRCAND logos are composed into RGBA; displaying
  their mask alone previously produced a white square. Unsupported operations are
  reported instead of being treated as a successful bitmap copy.
- Hatch patterns use boundary-clipped lines, retain islands and apply pattern dashes.
  Density/output budgets fall back to a reported tint. Solid and wide-polyline fills
  are opaque; independent fills cannot cancel each other through even-odd batching.
- Render ordinary and multileader connectors and arrowheads. Preserve simple linetype
  dash spacing and polyline bands, including bulges and joins.
- Bundle unmodified Arimo/Tinos Latin WOFF2 faces from Fontsource 5.3.0, with their OFL
  licenses. Package file inventory includes all three sidecar source files and notices.
- Share raster-path resolution across server, CLI and SVG. The viewer also reports
  decoding/network failures, and identifies the reference, handles and searched paths.
- Payload cache keys include the sidecar binary's size/mtime. Restart the server after
  rebuilding; fresh builds no longer reuse a payload from the old reader. Preferences
  and recent files were retained.
- Fixed theme switching: text resolved the new ink while vector batches retained old
  colours. Rebuilding batches on theme changes keeps paper-theme frames/arrows visible.
- Added a generated, synthetic detail drawing and end-to-end/pixel checks. Updated the
  standard viewer probe's injected text primitive to schema 8 after its old tuple
  caused non-finite bounds. No new lint rules were added.
