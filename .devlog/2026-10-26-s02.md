# 2026-10-26 / s02 — Findings say what is wrong and how to fix it in AutoCAD

Outcome: O10 · State: partial

## Result

Every finding now carries a plain-language headline (`title`), the evidence numbers
(`message`) and ordered fix steps (`fix`: AutoCAD command plus what it does). The viewer
shows the headline and evidence on each card; the selected card expands to **How to fix**
with **Copy AutoCAD selection** (AutoLISP that makes the finding's handles the pickfirst
selection, capped at 2,000) and **Copy handles**. The CLI prints the same three parts.

On the Dibrugarh network drawing (no profile) the count moved from 16 errors / 20 warnings
to 2 errors / 34 warnings: the 13 text-size errors rested on an assumed A0 sheet and are
now warnings that say the paper is not confirmed. The two remaining errors (scale label
1:100 against a border that would print 15.9 × 16.8 m at 1:100; INSUNITS millimetres on
projected-metre coordinates) hold whatever the paper.

Not done: rule on/off and severity overrides, accepted findings, paper in the GUI.
The AutoLISP selection string has not been tried in AutoCAD here (no AutoCAD on this
machine).

## Changes and decisions

- `Finding` gains optional `title` and `fix: FixStep[]` (`src/types.ts`, exported from
  the package). The engine fills a missing title from the rule title. Lint output is not
  the payload, so this needed no schema bump on its own.
- Payload schema 5 → 6: layers carry `plot` (false for no-plot layers and Defpoints)
  (`sidecar/Program.cs`, `src/types.ts`, `src/store.ts`). `text-height-paper` skips text on
  layers that are off, frozen or do not plot.
- `text-height-paper` raises errors only when the scale rests on a profile paper or a
  declared-scale fit; on an assumed or shape-guessed sheet it warns.
- `src/sheet.ts`: with no profile paper, a frame that fills an A0–A4 sheet (within 8%) at
  its declared scale takes that sheet (`paperSource: "declared"`), instead of the guessed
  sheet disagreeing with a consistent title block. `sheet-frame`'s non-ISO note is
  skipped when the paper is known.
- `scale-mismatch` states what the border would print at the declared scale versus the
  measured one, which tells the drafter which number is wrong.
- Fix steps name AutoCAD commands (INSUNITS, TEXTEDIT, SCALETEXT, LAYER, ERASE, OVERKILL,
  ETRANSMIT, EXTERNALREFERENCES, PROPERTIES, LAYTHW/LAYON, LAYDEL, MOVE) with a one-line
  explanation, as the user chose. Sewer rules get headlines and fixes that point to the
  design output rather than guessing which value is wrong.
- Every copy in the viewer (finding selection and handles, inspector handle and link) opens
  a popup at the top right of the drawing showing exactly what was copied, kept until its
  close button is pressed and replaced by the next copy. If the clipboard refuses, the
  popup says so and shows the text to copy by hand.
- The finding copy buttons are icons beside **How to fix** (`i-select`, `i-copy` in
  `viewer/index.html`); their tooltips name the action, the entity count and the
  AutoLISP requirement.
- Finding cards (Findings tab and the Inspect tab's "Findings on this entity") show
  severity as the coloured dot used on the filter chips, not the word; the dot carries a
  tooltip and an aria-label. `.sev-word` styles are gone.
