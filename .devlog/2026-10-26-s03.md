# 2026-10-26 / s03 — Corner toolbar, status-bar facts, edge ribbons

Outcome: O10 · State: partial
Source: this change · Environment: Linux x64, Node 25.8.0, headless Chromium

## Result

The canvas is less cluttered. Navigation (Back, Fit, zoom, background image) sits in the
bottom-right corner of the drawing, and the drawing facts (version, units, error/warning count)
sit in the status bar instead of the header. Each side panel collapses with a chevron on its
outer edge. A collapsed panel leaves a ribbon of its tabs on the window edge. The ribbon can be
dragged up or down, and clicking one of its tabs reopens the panel on that tab at its saved
width. The Browse/Recent divider in Files is now a visible rule with a grip.

This was the user's own direction for the chrome. It replaces the s02 handoff's reading
(toolbar into the header).

## Changes and decisions

- [viewer/index.html](../viewer/index.html): the panel-toggle buttons are gone. Ribbons
  `#ribbon-left` and `#ribbon-right` live in `#stage`, collapse buttons sit in each `.tabs`,
  and `#meta` has moved into `#status`.
- [viewer/main.ts](../viewer/main.ts): `showTab(side, tab)` opens the panel and keeps the
  panel tabs and ribbon tabs in sync, and `setPanel(side, open)` is the single open/close path.
  The ribbon position is a 0–1 fraction of the stage height, saved in the layout key as
  `ribbon-left` / `ribbon-right`. A press counts as a drag only after it moves 4 px, and the
  click that ends a drag does not open a tab. The entities chip was dropped from `#meta`
  because the status bar already shows the entity count.
- [viewer/style.css](../viewer/style.css): corner tools, the status-bar hint and the
  clipboard popup step 54 px clear of a visible ribbon. The ribbon is placed by pure CSS from
  `--pos`, so it stays inside the stage when the window resizes.
- [scripts/viewer-check.js](../scripts/viewer-check.js): the panel check now covers
  collapse → ribbon visible → ribbon tab reopens the panel on that pane.

## Tooltips (follow-up in the same session)

- Every interactive control now has a tooltip that says what it does, and where it applies, the
  shortcut or effect: header, tabs, ribbons, collapse buttons, filters, file rows, severity
  chips, packs (with their rule ids), profile line, AutoCAD commands in fix steps, inspect
  actions, status-bar fields and dialog buttons.
- The tips are a themed popover in [viewer/main.ts](../viewer/main.ts) driven by ordinary
  `title` attributes. On first hover a title moves to `data-tip` so the native tip does not
  double up, and it is kept as `aria-label`/`aria-description`. A trailing `(F)` / `(Alt + Left)`
  renders as a key cap. The tip shows after 450 ms, at once when moving between controls, and
  on keyboard focus. Ribbons place tips beside them (`data-tip-side`), and controls low on the
  screen get tips above. A popover sits in the top layer, so tips also show over dialogs.
- Checked with real CDP hover on Fit, Properties, the units badge, the collapse button, a
  ribbon tab, a layer row, a severity chip, the coordinates, a pack and the help-dialog close
  button. The tip opened with the right text and stayed on-screen. `--check` and `npm test`
  pass.
