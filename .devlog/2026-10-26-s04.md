# 2026-10-26 / s04 — Text codes stripped; text drawn at AutoCAD size, face and wrap

Outcome: O10 · State: partial
Source: this change · Environment: Linux x64, Node 25.8.0

## Result

MTEXT written as `{\W8.133788; }SEWER NETWORK…` (a space widened to indent a title) showed as
`W8.133788; SEWER NETWORK…`, so the title sat on top of the north arrow and the subtitle
ran over the pipe. That came from ACadSharp's `PlainText`, which leaves `\W` behind. 434
MTEXTs in `D2-D6-HSR LAYOUT - 900 & 300 mm-LSECTION-FP.dwg` were affected. Codes are now
stripped. In rendered text, a widened space becomes round(width) spaces, so the indents
are about right. The match against the issued PDF was not checked.

Text was drawn about 30% too small everywhere. AutoCAD's text height is the capital height,
but we used it as the font size. Measured on D1.1 (`BEP Submissions 01.12.2024/.../CAD/D1.1-HSR_LAYOUT_SECTION_-_900_&_300_mm.dwg`)
against its issued PDF: the title MTEXT has h = 26.81, and the PDF sets it in 66.02 pt Times
(`Tf` 2200.823 × 0.03). At 1.6705 pt/unit, that is an em of 39.5 units, or 1.47 h, which is
Times' em-to-cap ratio. Now the title spans almost the PDF's width, and manhole labels and
legend rows fit their boxes as in the PDF. TEXT `%%u`, `%%d`, `%%p`, `%%c` and `%%nnn` used to
show literally (`%%UNOTES:`); they are now decoded when drawn.

Title-block text ran out of its cells. MTEXT wasn't wrapped to its box width (group 41,
`RectangleWidth`): the D2-D6 project title is one paragraph in a 322,707-wide box at
h 6,405. Text was also drawn in one sans face, so Times and Arial Narrow styles came out too
wide. MTEXT now wraps, and text prims carry their face and width factor. The D2-D6 title
block (review status, project title, drawing name, notes) stays inside its cells in both
`render` and the viewer. The subtitle `{\W9.74885;` and 11 spaces`}L1- MH 34 - OUTFALL` sits
on one line, ending under the end of the title.

Still different from the PDF:
- **Embedded table:** the SECTION & MANHOLE INDEX on D1.1 is an Excel OLE2FRAME (`1A0B5`).
  Its bytes hold an `OlePres` stream with an EMF picture. We draw only the frame.
- **Faces and widths:** only sans, serif and the narrow squeeze are modelled. Inline MTEXT
  font switches (`\F`) and non-ASCII advances (taken as 556/1000 em) are approximate. Wrap
  points can differ from AutoCAD's by a word when a line is within a few percent of the box.

## Changes and decisions

- `sidecar/Program.cs`: `MTextPlain` replaces `MText.PlainText`. It handles `\P \X \~`,
  escapes, `\W` (scoped by braces), `\S` (shown as a/b), `\U+XXXX` and skips arg codes
  `\A \C \c \F \f \H \Q \T \p` and the switches `\L \O \K \N`. Query/lint text keeps a
  single space. Only rendered text (`EmitMText`) widens it, so label matching is unchanged.
- `src/render-svg.ts`: `xml:space="preserve"` on `<text>` so leading spaces survive in
  `render` output. The canvas viewer already keeps them.
- Text size: `viewer/canvas.ts` and `src/render-svg.ts` draw the em at h / 0.72 (sans cap
  height). They place the baseline at h (top) or h/2 (middle) below the anchor rather than
  using font baselines, because AutoCAD's top and middle refer to the capitals. Text bbox
  width went from 0.6 h to 0.8 h per character (`sidecar/Program.cs` `PrimText` and
  `primBounds` in canvas). No payload schema change.
- Schema 7 (`sidecar/Program.cs`, `src/types.ts`, `src/store.ts`, schema test): text prims
  end with `font` (`sans` | `serif`) and `wf`. `IsSerif` maps a style's font file or name
  (times, georgia, garamond, cambria, palatino, bookman, century) to serif. `FaceWidth`
  draws narrow and condensed faces (Arial Narrow, `arialn`) as the regular face times 0.82.
  `wf` also absorbs uneven block X/Y scale. The viewer and SVG draw serif with
  `"Times New Roman", Times, "Liberation Serif"` at em 1.47 h and apply `wf` as a horizontal
  scale.
- MTEXT wrap: greedy word wrap of each paragraph to `RectangleWidth / face`, measured with
  advance tables read from Liberation Sans and Serif (`/usr/share/fonts/liberation`, via a
  throwaway cmap/hmtx reader). The sans table matched Helvetica's AFM exactly. Indents stay
  on the first line. The sidecar text bbox uses the same widths.
- Widened spaces are summed before rounding: 11 × 9.74885 gives 107 spaces, not 110. The
  extra 3 spaces were about one capital height and pushed OUTFALL onto a third line.
- `TextPlain` in the sidecar decodes `%%` codes only for drawing. Query and lint still see the
  stored value, so no profile regex changes meaning.
